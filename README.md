## About Me

I am a **Senior Algorithm Expert** at Alibaba Cloud. I obtained my Ph.D. degree and B.S. degree both from Zhejiang University in 2018 and 2012, respectively.

My research focuses on **LLM post-training** technologies, spanning both academic research and industrial deployment.

## Research Interests

- **Reasoning & Agent:** Enhancing model reasoning and agent capabilities through knowledge distillation and Reinforcement Learning (RL).
- **Model Safety:** Building LLM safety guardrails to intercept multi-dimensional risks—including content violations, prompt injection, jailbreak attacks, and model hallucinations.

## Featured Projects
